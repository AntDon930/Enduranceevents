End the year with a run – with friends, in fancy dress or chasing a personal best, and then sit down to your New Year's Eve dinner with a clear conscience. Our list has more than 100 races on 30 and 31 December. Here are the best ones, all across Germany, Austria, Switzerland and South Tyrol.

Why a New Year's Eve run is so special

A New Year's Eve run is the most relaxed race of the year. Nobody has to set a personal best, many people come with the whole family or their running group, some in fancy dress. Most courses are short – 5 or 10 kilometres – perfect for beginners and for anyone who just wants to get out again after the holidays. And if you want to keep going through winter, a date on the 31st gives you a nice goal. Tips for that are in “Running in winter”.

For the long-distance fans

If you'd like to finish the year with a really long run, you'll find that too:

  • Erzsteig über Hüggel-Gipfel UltraTrail (Hasbergen near Osnabrück, 30 December): 30.8 and 46 km.

  • Silvesterlauf Gießen and CVJM Silvesterlauf in Netphen: both with a half marathon.

Even before New Year's Eve

Some New Year's Eve runs take place a few days earlier – handy if you're already busy on the 31st. The Zürcher Silvesterlauf is on 13 December; on 27 December you can run the 52. Internationale Sparverein Silvesterlauf Saarbrücken (10 km), the Altacher Silvesterlauf in Vorarlberg (6.2 and 12.4 km) and the Silvesterlauf Oeversee near Flensburg (5, 9 and 11 km).

Tip: New Year's Eve runs are popular, and some sell out early. If you have a favourite, better sign up now. And bring warm, dry clothes for afterwards – after crossing the finish line in December, you cool down quickly.

Conclusion

Whether it's a big city, a Baltic beach or a castle hill, 5 km or an ultra: at the turn of the year there's the right run for everyone. Pick one, bring your friends – and start the new year with a smile.
